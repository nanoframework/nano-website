### Components/Modules

  
Creators of the popular ESP32 chip series. They provide the RTOS, HAL and drivers that runs the ESP32 WROOM32 refence target.  
**nanoFramework** is part of ESP32 ecosystem, being one of the recommended [SDKs](https://www.espressif.com/en/support/download/sdk) to develop with ESP32.
