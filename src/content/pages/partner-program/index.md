.NET **nanoFramework** "Partner Program" is an extension to the core components, tools, and support channels. Aimed at commercial users developing .NET **nanoFramework** based projects has the objective to help them reduce their development effort and, consequently, the time to market. Partners are companies or individuals that offer their engineering services, tools, training, software components or hardware modules.

## Hardware Manufacturers

Hardware Manufacturers are companies that integrate .NET **nanoFramework** support into their development kits, catering to both hobbyists and commercial users. If you're a Hardware Manufacturer, it's advantageous to include .NET **nanoFramework** support in your products, as it can expand your customer base and enhance your offerings.
