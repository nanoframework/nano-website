.NET nanoFramework™'s preferred branding is the square logo together with our name.

Please note that we use a lower case 'n' at the start of nanoFramework, to emphasise the platform's goal of targeting very small and constrained devices.

The first occurrence of .NET nanoFramework in the body of any text must be followed by the trademark symbol, ™. A paragraph heading counts as part of the body of the text. After the first occurrence, the trademark symbol can be omitted.

The logo was designed by [Julina Pires](https://www.linkedin.com/in/julianampires). It represents tracks and via holes on a printed circuit board.

## Logos
